# Mrs. Davis

## Our Thoughts

This is a show that had the potential to be over-the-top. It was quirky, funny, and respectful of religion right up to the ending. And then they had to Hollywood it. For a show that all of the episodes treated religion respectfully, the very ending message was we didn't need Jesus. I just don't understand how they could have screwed the pooch on this so hard.

## IMDB Summary

Sister Simone partners with her ex-boyfriend Wiley on a globe-spanning journey to destroy Mrs. Davis, a powerful artificial intelligence.
