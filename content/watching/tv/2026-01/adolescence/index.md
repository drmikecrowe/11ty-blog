# Adolescence

## Our Thoughts

I dreaded watching this show because I knew how heavy it was. It is amazing. Stephen Graham is phenomenal. The fact that he wrote this as well is just icing on the cake. You'll notice the entire episode is one take -- I read that they sometimes took 15 takes.

## IMDB Summary

When a 13-year-old is accused of the murder of a classmate, his family, therapist and the detective in charge are all left asking what really happened.
