# Blue Moon

## Our Thoughts

As a testament to how good this movie is, consider that I'm not a fan of Ethan Hawke. But this movie was gripping not only in how well he did, but in just how much he memorized to portray Lorenz Hart. Andrew Scott is amazing as always. Don't get me wrong, this is a movie about the struggles with alcoholism and mental health as he tries to save face watching his partner move on and be successful.

## IMDB Summary

Tells the story of Lorenz Hart's struggles with alcoholism and mental health as he tries to save face during the opening of "Oklahoma!".
