# One Battle After Another

## IMDB

- **Actors:** Leonardo DiCaprio, Sean Penn
- **Director:** Paul Thomas Anderson
- **Year:** 2025

## Summary

When their evil enemy resurfaces after 16 years, a group of ex-revolutionaries reunite to rescue the daughter of one of their own.

## Our Thoughts

I'm not a fan of Leonardo DiCaprio, but I still enjoyed this movie. It is a wild ride that you really don't know where it's going or how it's going to end up. But kudos to Sean Penn for his transformation and where in the hell did he learn to walk like that for this movie?

But with all that, hats off to Benicio Del Toro for his performance. He made it fantastic.
