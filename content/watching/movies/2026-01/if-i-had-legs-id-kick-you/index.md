# If I Had Legs I'd Kick You

## Our Thoughts

Don't get me wrong, Rose Byrne did phenomenal and you could just feel her pain and struggles. But this movie was hard to watch and get all the way through. She won at the Critics' Choice Awards and I understand why, but man, I'm not sure I'd want to recommend this.

But, that's A24 for you, folks. May be great, may be just weird.

## IMDB Summary

While trying to manage her own life and career, a woman on the verge of a breakdown must cope with her daughter's illness, an absent husband, a missing person, and an unusual relationship with her therapist.
