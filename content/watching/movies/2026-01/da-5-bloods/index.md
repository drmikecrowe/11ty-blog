# Da 5 Bloods

## Our Thoughts

Let me start by saying I'm a huge fan of Delroy Lindo, and I'd watch him read the phone book. I didn't realize this was Spike Lee, nor that it was almost three hours, and I wish I had. Knowing what I know now, I'm not sure if I'd watch this again. Too much exposition and just over the top, jump the whale plot.

## Summary

Four black veterans battle the forces of man and nature when they return to Vietnam seeking the remains of their fallen squad leader and the gold fortune he helped them hide.
