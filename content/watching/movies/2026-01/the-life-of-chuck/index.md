# The Life of Chuck

## IMDB

- **Actors:** Tom Hiddleston, Jacob Tremblay
- **Director:** Mike Flanagan
- **Writers:** Mike Flanagan, Stephen King
- **Year:** 2024

## Summary

A life-affirming, genre-bending story about three chapters in the life of an ordinary man named Charles Krantz.

## Our Thoughts

We are a huge fan of Mike Flanagan. Hill House, Midnight Mass, were all fantastic Netflix specials.  I don't even remember how I found this, but I went into it not knowing what it was, and I think that confused me.

Understand going into it that the three chapters in Chuck's life are told from the end to the beginning. So things won't make sense until the very end.

Tom Hiddleston is amazing. And this story was masterfully crafted and presented. I can't stop thinking about it.
