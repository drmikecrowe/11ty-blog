## IMDB

- **Actors:** Stephen McHattie, Lisa Houle

## Summary

A radio host interprets the possible outbreak of a deadly virus which infects the small Ontario town he is stationed in.

## Our Thoughts

This movie has serious vibes similar to The Vast of Night, a low budget horror that takes place in an AM radio station. It was fantastic. I don't know what else to say other than really worth watching.
