# Hallow Road

## Our Thoughts

This is a real heady horror piece that actually stuck with us, so it is definitely worth watching. The entire movie takes place inside a car with two parents communicating with their daughter who's been in an accident. It's great drama, excellent acting, fantastic story, and totally worth it.

## IMDB Summary

Two parents enter a race against time when they receive a distressing late-night phone call from their daughter after she caused a tragic car accident.
